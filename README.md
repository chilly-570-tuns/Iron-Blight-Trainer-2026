## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Iron Blight**. This trainer provides optional quality-of-life advantages for Iron Blight's single-player campaign, including enhanced survivability, ammunition control, faster movement, and easier resource management. It is designed around the game's health, ammunition, weapons, inventory, food, medical supplies, exploration, and puzzle systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more accessible single-player experience, a relaxed way to explore Prasnova, or additional assistance with scarce ammunition, limited inventory space, environmental puzzles, and hostile encounters.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Launch Iron Blight and load a save before activating movement-related options. Use the movement speed control to move more quickly through outdoor streets and claustrophobic interiors while preserving normal interaction and exploration controls.

### Tools & Inventory

Iron Blight uses a limited grid-based inventory, and food, medical supplies, ammunition, tools, and weapons compete for available space. Activate unlimited item quantities when you want to conserve supplies, then manage the inventory normally through the game's own interface.

### World & Environment

Explore the rain-soaked streets and enclosed locations of Prasnova, search for tools, solve environmental puzzles, and investigate the surroundings while pursuing Valeria. Movement and inventory options can reduce logistical pressure without changing the game's locations or objectives.

### Combat & Weapons

Iron Blight emphasizes scarce ammunition, deliberate weapon handling, manual magazine checks, step-by-step reloading, and dangerous enemies that may be attracted by gunfire. Activate health protection, infinite ammunition, or no-reload options before an encounter when you want a less punishing firefight.
